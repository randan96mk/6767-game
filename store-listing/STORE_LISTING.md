## Full Description (max 4000 chars)
```
Challenge your brain with the classic 2048 number puzzle game!

Swipe in any direction to slide tiles across the board. When two tiles with the same number collide, they merge into one tile with double the value. Start with 2s and 4s, and keep merging to reach the legendary 2048 tile!

HOW TO PLAY
• Swipe up, down, left, or right to move all tiles
• Matching tiles merge when they collide (2+2=4, 4+4=8, and so on)
• A new tile (2 or 4) appears after each move
• Plan your moves carefully — the board fills up fast!
• Reach the 2048 tile to win, then keep going for a higher score

FEATURES
• Classic 2048 gameplay — simple rules, deep strategy
• Beautiful dark cyberpunk neon theme with glowing tiles
• Smooth animations on every merge and spawn
• Score tracking with all-time best score saved
• Undo your last move if you make a mistake
• Continue playing after reaching 2048 to chase higher tiles
• Move counter to track your efficiency
• Works completely offline — no internet needed
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no data collection
• Lightweight and battery-friendly

TILE PROGRESSION
2 → 4 → 8 → 16 → 32 → 64 → 128 → 256 → 512 → 1024 → 2048 and beyond!

Each tile has its own unique color in the cyberpunk palette — from cool blues for small tiles to glowing cyan, vibrant magenta, and brilliant gold for the 2048 tile.

PERFECT FOR
• Quick brain-training sessions
• Waiting rooms and commutes
• Anyone who loves number puzzles
• Fans of the original 2048

PRIVACY
This app collects zero data. No analytics, no ads, no tracking. Your best score is saved locally on your device and never leaves it.

Download now and see if you can reach 2048!
```
